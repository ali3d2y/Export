Who we are

Bridging Indian Excellence with the World

Iconic Global Exporters is a trusted export house specializing in high-quality rice, farm-fresh onions, bananas, and finely crafted brass items for domestic and international buyers.

We source our agricultural goods directly from reliable farmers — ensuring freshness, purity, and consistent grading — while our brass items are crafted by skilled artisans using traditional techniques and premium materials.

With rigorous quality control, export-standard packaging, and a strong commitment to ethical trade, we build long-term partnerships rooted in trust and transparency.
